# Wii Sports Resort

## What is this game?

Wii Sports Resort is a 2009 Wii sports collection set on Wuhu Island, featuring
Swordplay, Wakeboarding, Frisbee, Archery, Basketball, Table Tennis, Golf,
Bowling, Power Cruising, Canoeing, Cycling, and Air Sports.

## Where is the settings page?

The [player options page](../player-options) lets you configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Nearly everything starts locked. Gamemodes, Swordplay Showdown stages, Cycling
routes, Golf and Frisbee Golf courses, Power Cruising courses, and the Archery,
Wakeboarding, and Canoeing difficulty tiers are all shuffled into the item pool.
You begin with a single random gamemode and, when needed, one random compatible
stage/course/time unlock. Open up the rest as you receive items.

## What is the goal?

Configurable: unlock all 17 gamemodes (default), unlock all 20 Swordplay
Showdown stages, collect every Island Flyover iPoint, earn every stamp in a
chosen category, or earn all 100 stamps.

## What are the location checks?

- **Stamps** — the game's 100 achievement stamps.
- **Island Flyover iPoints** — 80 points of interest around Wuhu Island.
- **Swordplay Showdown clears** — clearing any of the 20 stages.

## What "items" can appear in another player's world?

Gamemode, stage, course, and difficulty unlocks, plus Progressive Cycling
Stamina Upgrades, Progressive Swordplay Showdown Heart Upgrades, and
Progressive Table Tennis Can Score Upgrades. Everything else is filler
(`Nothing`). (Please help us come up with more ideas for filler other than nothing.)
